1. What Cookies Are

A cookie is a small text file a website stores in your browser. It lets the site recognise your browser on later requests. Related technologies such as local storage work similarly but keep data in the browser rather than sending it with every request.

2. Cookies We Use

{{ brand_name }} uses only the cookies needed to run the Service. We do not use advertising cookies, cross-site tracking cookies, or third-party analytics cookies.

NameCategoryPurposeDuration
sessionid Strictly necessary Keeps you signed in between requests. Marked HttpOnly and SameSite=Lax. Approximately 14 days
csrftoken Strictly necessary Protects forms against cross-site request forgery. Session, or up to 1 year with "remember me"
ui_lang Functional Remembers whether you chose the Chinese or English interface. 1 year

Strictly necessary cookies cannot be switched off without breaking sign-in and form submission. The functional cookie can be cleared at any time; the only effect is that the interface falls back to its default language.

3. Local Storage

The Service stores your sidebar collapsed/expanded preference in your browser's local storage under the key sidebarCollapsed. This data stays in your browser, is not transmitted to us, and is cleared when you clear site data.

4. Third-Party Cookies

We do not set third-party cookies ourselves. If your deployment enables optional integrations (for example a bot-protection or error-monitoring service), that provider may set its own cookies; in that case they are listed in the table above after review. Content you load from Connected Platforms — embedded posts, for instance — is governed by those platforms' own cookie policies.

5. Do Not Track

Because we do not track you across sites for advertising, the Service does not respond differently to browser "Do Not Track" signals. We do not sell or share personal information as defined by the CCPA/CPRA.

6. Managing Cookies

You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings. Blocking sessionid or csrftoken will prevent you from signing in and using the Service. Most browsers also offer a private/incognito mode that discards cookies when you close the window.

7. Changes to This Policy

If we add or remove cookies, we will update the table above and revise the effective date. Material changes will be announced in the Service or by email.
