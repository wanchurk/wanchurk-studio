3. Data We Collect

3.1 Account and organisation data

Name, email address, password hash (or single-sign-on identifier), profile picture if you provide one, organisation and workspace names, membership and role assignments, invitation records, and the timestamps at which you accepted these policies.

3.2 Connected platform data

When you connect a social media account, the platform returns an access token and often a refresh token, together with account identifiers, display names, and the permissions (scopes) you granted. Tokens are stored encrypted. While a connection is active, we also receive and store the content the platform sends us on your behalf: posts and their metadata, comments, mentions, direct messages, reviews, and performance metrics.

3.3 Content you submit

Drafts, captions, media files, approval comments, and any other material you or your team upload or write in the Service.

We do not collect payment card details, because the Service is currently provided free of charge. We do not buy personal data from data brokers, and we do not use your content to train machine learning models.

8. How Long We Keep Data

  • Account and workspace data — for as long as your organisation exists in the Service.
  • After deletion — organisation deletion runs through a grace period of approximately 14 days, during which you can cancel; after it elapses the data is removed from active systems and from backups on the provider's normal backup-rotation schedule.
  • Publish audit records — retained for up to 90 days to support troubleshooting and abuse investigation.
  • Session records — expire after approximately 14 days of inactivity.

  • Security and access logs — retained only as long as needed for security and legal obligations.
