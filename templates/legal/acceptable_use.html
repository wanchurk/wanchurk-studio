This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") sets out what you may not do with {{ brand_name }}. It forms part of our Terms of Service. It applies to everyone who accesses the Service, including members you invite and clients you give portal access to.

1. Prohibited Content

You may not use the Service to create, upload, publish, or distribute content that:

  • is unlawful, defamatory, fraudulent, or misleading;
  • infringes copyright, trademark, or other intellectual property rights;
  • contains malware, phishing, or deceptive links;
  • sexualises minors, or depicts child sexual abuse material — which we report to the authorities;
  • incites violence, hatred, or discrimination against a protected group;
  • harasses, threatens, or intimidates a person;
  • discloses another person's private information without consent;
  • promotes the sale of illegal goods, prescription drugs without a valid licence, weapons, or counterfeit products; or
  • violates any Connected Platform's content policies.

2. Prohibited Conduct

  • Spam and bulk unsolicited messaging. No mass DMs, comment spam, engagement-bait schemes, or automated outreach to people who did not ask for it.
  • Circumventing platform rules. No evading rate limits, bans, or moderation by rotating accounts, proxies, or credentials.
  • Fake engagement. No buying followers, likes, views, or reviews, and no operating engagement pods.
  • Impersonation. No pretending to be a person, brand, or organisation you do not represent.
  • Unauthorised access. No accessing accounts, tokens, or data you were not given permission to use, and no probing, scanning, or testing the Service's security without our written consent.
  • Overloading. No activity that degrades the Service for others, including deliberate high-volume API calls outside documented limits.
  • Resale without permission. No reselling or white-labelling the Service as your own product without a separate written agreement with us.
  • Misuse of client access. No sharing portal links beyond their intended recipient.

3. Platform-Specific Requirements

Each Connected Platform imposes its own rules, and connecting an account means you accept them. In particular you must comply with the Meta Platform Terms and Developer Policies, the LinkedIn API Terms, the TikTok Developer Terms, the YouTube API Services Terms, the Pinterest Developer Terms, and the terms of any other platform you connect.

Where a platform restricts how its data may be stored or displayed — for example caching limits or deletion obligations — those restrictions apply to your use of the Service as well, and you agree to respect them.

4. Messaging and Automation

Automated replies are permitted only in response to a person who initiated contact, and only where the platform allows it. You must not use automation to send unsolicited commercial messages, to evade a platform's messaging window, or to contact people who opted out.

5. Security

You must keep credentials confidential, use a unique password, and not attempt to access another organisation's workspaces. Report suspected vulnerabilities to {{ legal_contact_email }} rather than disclosing them publicly.

6. Enforcement

We investigate suspected breaches. Depending on severity we may remove content, throttle access, suspend the affected connection, or suspend or terminate your account. Where the law requires it, or where content is clearly illegal, we may report it to the relevant authorities without notice to you.

We aim to act proportionately and, where practical, to give notice and a chance to remedy the issue before suspending access.

7. Reporting Abuse

To report a breach of this policy, email {{ legal_contact_email }} with the relevant account, workspace, and a description of the conduct.

8. Changes

We may update this policy as platforms change their rules. Material changes will be announced in the Service or by email before they take effect.
